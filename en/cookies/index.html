Cookie policy

The current landing site uses minimal technical storage. We do not want the website to depend on unnecessary tracking, so this page explains what is stored, why it is stored, and how you can remove it.

Last updated: 7 April 2026 Minimal technical storage
Current use

Remembering your cookie choice

The site uses technical browser storage to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie banner. This prevents the same banner from being shown on every page load.

The current landing page does not run ad-tracking scripts. If additional analytics or embedded services are introduced later, this policy will be updated accordingly.
Categories

Technical, not advertising-focused

  • Remembering the consent choice shown in the cookie banner.
  • Basic platform or browser-level storage required for normal rendering.
  • Supportive website operation, not marketing profiling.
Control

How to clear or reset it

  • Clear site data or browser storage for this domain.
  • Use a private browsing session if you do not want the choice stored locally.
  • Reset your browser storage to see the cookie banner again.
