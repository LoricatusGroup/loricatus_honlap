Privacy notice

This page explains, in practical terms, how Loricatus handles the data submitted through contact and quote forms. The goal is a proportionate process that supports project discussions without collecting unnecessary information.

Last updated: 7 April 2026 Contact: info@loricatus.hu
Controller

Loricatus

Loricatus is the data controller for contact requests and quote submissions received through this website. We use the information only to review the enquiry, respond, and move a relevant project discussion forward.

Website form messages are routed through a form-processing provider for delivery. The provider only receives the data needed to forward the request.
Data we collect

Contact and project details

  • Name, email address, phone number, and optionally company name.
  • The selected service area.
  • The project summary and any context you include in the message.

We intentionally limit the fields to what is needed for a first response or a relevant quote discussion.

Purpose

Response, coordination, preparation

  • To process quote requests and answer enquiries.
  • To coordinate follow-up calls, meetings, or site discussions.
  • To maintain an internal record of active business communication.

The legal basis is typically your consent and, after contact has been initiated, our legitimate interest in maintaining the project conversation.

Retention and rights

Kept only as long as needed

  • We keep enquiry data only for as long as the business discussion requires it.
  • You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.
  • You may ask what data we currently hold in relation to your enquiry.

Privacy-related requests can be sent to info@loricatus.hu and we will respond as quickly as reasonably possible.
