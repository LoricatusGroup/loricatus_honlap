Case studies

Completed work,
with real results

A few selected projects: what we solved, the results we achieved, and why the solution was valuable to the client.

BIM coordination

NMHH EMC Lab – BIM documentation

Full drone and laser survey of an existing industrial lab, followed by an IFC-based BIM model and CDE integration.

Result

Millimetre-accurate as-built survey · complete IFC export · design lead time cut by ~30%.

Why it matters

The client works from a single, reliable digital source – fewer site visits, fewer design errors, faster construction.

Building inspection

Industrial chimney – thermographic inspection

Condition survey of a hard-to-reach industrial chimney by drone, combining LiDAR and a thermal camera – with no scaffolding.

Result

Detailed defect map · survey completed in 1 day with no shutdown · documented thermal bridges and cracks.

Why it matters

The risk of scaffolding and work at height is gone, and maintenance can be planned precisely around the real defects.

3D drone survey

Matthias Church – 3D drone survey

Drone photogrammetry of a heritage façade to produce a high-resolution 3D point cloud and model, as part of the Hauszmann Programme.

Result

High-resolution 3D point cloud · heritage documentation · accurate base data for further design.

Why it matters

Fragile, hard-to-reach surfaces are documented contact-free and faithfully – a solid basis for restoration decisions.
