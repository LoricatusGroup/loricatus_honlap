The Loricatus difference

Where data becomes
usable reality

At Loricatus, our job is to make the built world more accurate and more understandable. Sometimes it is straightforward. Sometimes it is not. Every project improves the next one.

We do more than fly drones. We build reliable digital context for design, construction, inspection, and handover. Our team combines engineering, surveying, and BIM coordination expertise, from the first site capture to the final deliverable. Whether the task is point-cloud surveying, asset inspection, or model coordination, data is not decoration here. It is the working basis.

Full regulatory compliance Hungarian and EU drone rules, aviation permits, and insurance aligned to project requirements
Project-specific processing Data processing and delivery schedules are matched to the real complexity and decisions behind each assignment
Measured, documented outputs Detailed reports, point clouds, CAD exports, and GIS datasets that can be acted on immediately
